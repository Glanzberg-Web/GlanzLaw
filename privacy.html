Website Privacy Notice

Effective Date: October 15, 2026
Website: https://glanzlaw.com/

Glanzberg Law Firm, PLLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Notice explains how personal information may be collected, used, disclosed, and retained in connection with our website and related website inquiries. It also describes how to contact us about your information.

This Notice does not replace an engagement agreement or govern every aspect of information handled during legal representation. Nothing in this Notice limits our applicable professional duties concerning client or prospective-client information.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you contact us using the email address or telephone number listed on this website, we receive information you choose to provide. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, mailing address, and the contents of your inquiry. If you later provide documents through a separately agreed channel, those materials may also become part of your communications with the firm.

The website currently provides direct email and telephone contact details; it does not offer an active online contact form, appointment booking, document upload, newsletter signup, online payment feature, or website chat.

Please provide only information reasonably necessary for an initial inquiry. Do not send Social Security numbers, financial account information, medical records, confidential documents, or other sensitive information through an unsolicited email or a general website communication. Contact us first to discuss an appropriate method for providing sensitive information.

Information Collected Automatically

This website is delivered using GitHub Pages. As part of operating and securing a hosted website, GitHub or other infrastructure providers involved in delivering it may process technical information associated with requests, such as IP addresses, browser and device information, page requests, referring information, and request times. The provider’s collection and retention practices are subject to its applicable policies. You can review GitHub’s privacy statement.

How We Use Information

Subject to applicable professional obligations, we use information supplied in inquiries to respond to requests, communicate with you, assess whether we may assist with a potential legal matter, and conduct conflict checks when appropriate. Information may also be used as necessary to administer and protect the firm’s communications and comply with applicable legal and professional requirements.

Providing contact information for an inquiry does not itself constitute consent to receive marketing text messages or automated marketing calls.

When We Disclose Information

Subject to applicable legal and professional obligations, information associated with website visits and inquiries may be received or processed by providers involved in delivering the website or the communication, including GitHub Pages and any applicable email or telecommunications provider. Information also may be disclosed when you direct or authorize disclosure, or when required or permitted by applicable law and consistent with our professional obligations, including lawful legal process.

Third-party providers may have their own information-handling practices and privacy policies. This Notice does not represent that every such provider acts solely on the firm’s instructions.

Cookies, Analytics, and Other Tracking

The current website code does not include a firm-installed analytics platform, advertising pixel, embedded chat service, or tracking script. We have not implemented a website cookie-preference tool. Hosting and network providers may nevertheless use cookies, logs, or similar technologies as described in their own notices. This statement concerns the current website implementation and may change if additional services are introduced.

Browser controls may allow you to block or delete cookies. The website does not currently offer a separate on-site preference control for tracking signals.

Sale, Sharing, and Targeted Advertising

The current website does not contain an advertising-pixel or targeted-advertising integration. Questions concerning any information disclosure or privacy choices may be directed to the firm using the contact information below. The firm will review this notice if its website services or data-handling practices change.

Retention

Personal information may be retained for the time reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Notice, taking into account the nature of the information, our relationship with you, and applicable legal, professional, recordkeeping, and dispute-resolution requirements. Different retention periods may apply to website technical records, unsuccessful inquiries, conflict-check records, and client files. The retention of technical information by third-party service providers may be governed by their own practices.

Security

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please contact the firm to arrange an appropriate method for transmitting sensitive information rather than sending it in an unsolicited email.

Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may contact us using the information below to ask about personal information you have provided, request a correction, or make another privacy-related request. Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have additional rights concerning access, deletion, correction, or particular uses or disclosures of information.

Requests will be considered in light of applicable law and the firm’s professional obligations. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting on a request. Certain records may need to be retained to satisfy legal or professional obligations or protect the rights of others.

Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may link to third-party websites or services, including court websites. Those parties may handle information under their own privacy notices. We encourage you to review their policies before providing information. This does not eliminate any obligations we may have concerning information we disclose to those parties.

Children

This website presents information about legal services for a general audience and is not designed as a service for children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information through a communication with the firm, please contact us using the information below.

Website Inquiries and Attorney-Client Relationships

Visiting this website or sending an unsolicited inquiry does not, by itself, establish an attorney-client relationship. Please do not send confidential or sensitive details until we have confirmed that we can receive them and have provided an appropriate means of communication. This statement does not waive or limit any confidentiality duties that may apply under governing law or professional rules.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this Notice to reflect changes in our practices or applicable requirements. The revised Notice will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Where additional notice or consent is required by applicable law, we will provide or seek it as required. Website use alone does not constitute consent where separate consent is required.

Website Privacy

Website Privacy Notice

Glanzberg Law Firm, PLLC respects the privacy of individuals who visit this website or contact the firm. This notice describes the general information involved in using this website and contacting the firm directly.

Information You Provide

If you email or telephone the firm, you may provide your name, contact details, and information about an inquiry. The firm may use that information to respond, evaluate whether it can assist, conduct necessary conflict checks, and manage the inquiry.

Website Hosting and Technical Information

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may collect technical information, such as an IP address, browser details, and requests made to the website, for operation, security, and troubleshooting. That information is handled under the provider’s own policies. Learn more in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact and Confidentiality

Sending an email, making a telephone call, or using this website does not by itself establish an attorney-client relationship. Please do not send confidential, privileged, or time-sensitive information until the firm confirms that it can receive that information and an appropriate relationship has been established.

Website Contact Form

Online form submissions are not currently enabled. The website directs inquiries to the firm’s published email address and telephone number. This notice will be reviewed if a contact form or additional processing services are activated.

Questions About Privacy

For questions concerning this website or information you have provided, contact jack@glanzlaw.com or call (718) 569-7757.

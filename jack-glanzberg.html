Attorney Profile

A litigation practice built around coverage, property and complex civil disputes.

Jack Glanzberg is the founder and managing partner of Glanzberg Law Firm, PLLC. Public professional materials describe more than a decade of litigation experience, with a primary focus on insurance coverage and property damage litigation.

His practice also includes contractor negligence, premises liability, personal injury, homeowners and commercial insurance matters, and related civil litigation. Public court records reflect his work in New York state and federal matters and, more recently, appearances through Glanzberg Law Firm, PLLC.

Jack earned his J.D. from the Elisabeth Haub School of Law at Pace University. He has also publicly identified himself as a certified mediator.

Founding Partner

Jack Glanzberg, Esq.

Profile

Insurance, property-damage, and civil litigation experience.

Mr. Glanzberg began his career in the field of law at the age of 14 working in the mail room of what is now a preeminent personal injury firm. Mr. Glanzberg is currently a solo practitioner and litigator representing insureds and insurance carriers in insurance coverage or property damage related disputes, both commercial and residential. With over 15 years of experience in the field, Mr. Glanzberg is well versed in insurance coverage and property damage related issues, including but not limited to 1st Party Claims, 3rd Party Claims, Errors and Omissions, Declaratory Judgment, Liability Coverage, Broker Negligence, and Contractor Negligence. Mr. Glanzberg is also experienced in cases involving Personal Injury, Labor Law, F.L.S.A. claims, and general litigation disputes. In representing both insureds and insurance carriers, plaintiffs and defendants, Mr. Glanzberg is uniquely situated to understand the perspective of both sides, making him an ideal mediator for any dispute.

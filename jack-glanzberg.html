Profile

Experience built in the disputes the firm now handles.

Jack describes himself publicly as a litigator with more than a decade of experience in property-damage litigation and insurance-related matters.

He founded Glanzberg Law Firm, PLLC with a core practice in insurance coverage and property-damage litigation. His public professional profile also identifies experience involving personal injury, premises liability, contractor negligence, commercial insurance, and homeowners insurance.

Public court records reflect Jack’s work in New York state and appellate courts, the federal courts in New York, and federal and state matters in New Jersey. The current firm’s docketed work includes insurance-contract and property-loss disputes for individuals and businesses.

A focused practice can be an advantage when the dispute turns on dense policy language, technical facts, and litigation strategy.

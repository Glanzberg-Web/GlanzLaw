About the Firm

Litigation experience with a practical, client-focused approach.

The firm handles disputes from early claim evaluation through motion practice, trial preparation and appeal, with particular experience in insurance coverage and property-loss matters.

Clients often come to the firm when a coverage position, denied claim, construction loss or business dispute has become too complex to resolve informally. The goal is to identify the controlling legal and factual issues early, build a clear record, and pursue the most effective path toward resolution.

Founder & Managing Partner

Jack Glanzberg, Esq.

Jack Glanzberg is a litigator whose practice centers on insurance coverage, property damage and related civil disputes. He founded Glanzberg Law Firm, PLLC after more than a decade of litigation experience.

His publicly described practice includes commercial and homeowners insurance, property disputes, personal injury, premises liability and contractor negligence. He has also served as counsel in New York appellate matters and identifies himself as a certified mediator.

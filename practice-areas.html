Practice Areas

Coverage, property, and civil disputes.

The firm’s practice is centered on insurance coverage and property-damage litigation, with related civil matters and dispute resolution work.

Insurance

Insurance Coverage & Property Claims

Disputes over whether, and to what extent, an insurance policy responds to a claimed loss.

Publicly documented matters handled by Glanzberg Law Firm include first-party property and commercial insurance disputes. These cases can turn on policy wording, exclusions, causation, valuation, notice, and the underlying facts of the loss.

  • Denied or limited property claims
  • Policy interpretation and exclusion disputes
  • Commercial and homeowners insurance matters
  • Declaratory-judgment and indemnification disputes
  • Pre-suit claim analysis and litigation
Property

Property Damage Litigation

When a property loss becomes a legal dispute, the issues often extend beyond the amount of damage.

The firm handles disputes involving damaged real property, business property, inventory, and losses connected to construction, contractors, neighboring property, or insurance coverage.

  • Building and premises damage
  • Business-property and inventory losses
  • Construction-related property disputes
  • Contractor and third-party negligence claims
  • Disputes involving responsibility for the loss
Civil Litigation

Related Civil Litigation

Jack’s public professional profile identifies personal injury, premises liability, contractor negligence, and related litigation among his areas of experience.

Depending on the facts, these matters may involve overlapping contract, tort, insurance, and indemnification questions.

  • Contractor negligence
  • Premises and property liability
  • Personal injury matters
  • Commercial and contract disputes
  • Insurance-related defense and indemnification issues

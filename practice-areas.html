What We Handle

Practice Areas

Focused civil litigation and dispute-resolution services with an emphasis on insurance coverage and property-related claims.

Property Damage Insurance Claims & Litigation

Representation in disputes involving covered property losses, claim valuation, denials, exclusions, causation, examinations under oath and related coverage issues.

Insurance Coverage & Indemnification

Coverage analysis and litigation involving duties to defend and indemnify, additional-insured issues, declaratory-judgment actions and policy interpretation.

Contractor & Construction Negligence

Claims arising from construction damage, defective work, adjacent-property impacts, negligent contractors and related risk-transfer questions.

General & Commercial Litigation

Business disputes, contract claims, motion practice, trial preparation and other civil litigation matters for individuals and commercial clients.

Broker & Agent Negligence

Claims concerning insurance procurement, inaccurate applications, requested coverage that was not obtained and other broker or agent errors.

Defense Representation

Defense of individuals and businesses facing civil claims, including matters where insurance coverage may be disputed or unavailable.

Premises & Property Liability

Representation in property-related liability disputes, including premises claims and matters involving owners, tenants, contractors and insurers.

Mediation & Dispute Resolution

Neutral and advocacy services designed to resolve disputes efficiently where litigation is not the only or best path forward.

Practice Areas

Insurance, property, and civil litigation.

Glanzberg Law Firm represents insureds, insurance carriers, property owners, businesses, plaintiffs, and defendants in coverage, property-damage, and related civil disputes.

Insurance

Property Damage Insurance Claims & Litigation

Representation in residential and commercial property-loss disputes from claim evaluation through litigation.

These matters may involve fire, water, collapse, wind, theft, vandalism, business interruption, and other covered losses, along with disputes over policy interpretation, exclusions, causation, valuation, and the scope of coverage.

Construction

Adjacent Property Construction

Claims arising when neighboring excavation, underpinning, shoring, demolition, renovation, or construction activity causes damage to adjoining property. Depending on the policy language and facts, recovery may involve both insurance coverage issues and direct claims against responsible parties.

Coverage

Liability Coverage — Defense & Indemnification

Coverage disputes involving an insurer’s duty to defend or indemnify, additional-insured status, contractual risk transfer, exclusions, and other questions affecting who must respond to a claim or lawsuit.

Defense

Defense Representation

Representation of defendants in civil litigation, including property, personal-injury, premises, employment-related, and other contested matters where focused litigation strategy and insurance considerations may overlap.

Insurance Professionals

Broker Negligence

Claims involving insurance brokers or agents, including allegations that requested coverage was not properly procured, applications were mishandled, or available coverage did not match the insured’s stated needs.

Construction

Contractor Negligence

Property-damage and related claims arising from allegedly defective, negligent, or improper construction work, including situations in which faulty-workmanship exclusions or third-party liability issues affect insurance recovery.

Liability Claims

Direct Claims Against Third-Party Liability Carriers

Matters involving recovery from the liability insurer of a third party alleged to have caused a loss, including disputes over responsibility, available coverage, settlement, and the relationship between first-party and third-party claims.

Civil Litigation

General & Commercial Litigation

Contract disputes, business controversies, personal injury, Labor Law and F.L.S.A. matters, declaratory-judgment actions, and other civil litigation for plaintiffs and defendants in state and federal court.

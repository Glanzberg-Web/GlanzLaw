Current firm

Published decisions naming Glanzberg Law Firm, PLLC.

The two matters below are especially useful because the published decisions expressly identify the firm and Jack as counsel.

2026 NY Slip Op 04204

On Point Window Treatment, Inc. v. 208 Clifton Place, LLC

Appellate Division, Second Department · July 1, 2026

Glanzberg Law Firm, PLLC, with Jack Glanzberg, Esq. of counsel, represented the respondent in an action that included claims for injury to property arising from a commercial lease. The appellant challenged the denial of a CPLR 3211(a)(1) motion to dismiss.

Publicly reported disposition: the Appellate Division affirmed the order, with costs.

View official decision
2025 NY Slip Op 52113(U)

Oh Nuts Inc. v. Cincinnati Insurance Co.

Supreme Court, Kings County · December 24, 2025

Glanzberg Law Firm, PLLC, with Jack Glanzberg, Esq. of counsel, represented the plaintiff in a dispute over insurance coverage for damaged gift baskets and inventory, including the application of a product-damage exclusion.

Publicly reported disposition: the court denied Cincinnati Insurance Company’s motion to dismiss the complaint.

View official decision
